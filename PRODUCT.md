# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: hackathon judges (PayPal AI Hackathon on Devpost) watching a ~3-minute demo video and then clicking through the merchant console themselves. They need each screen to explain itself: why a dispute got its verdict, what evidence it rests on, and exactly which PayPal call will go out. Secondary: a merchant's dispute operator reviewing and approving recommended actions.

## Product Purpose

Provenant seals a signed, tamper-evident evidence bundle for every order an AI shopping agent places through PayPal (the user's signed mandate, the approved cart, a snapshot of the offer the agent saw, the agent's decision trace), linked to the PayPal order through `purchase_units[].custom_id`. When PayPal reports a dispute on that order, Provenant judges the buyer's claim against the bundle, assigns fault with one of five verdicts, and, after the merchant approves, takes the matching action through the PayPal Disputes API. Success: a judge understands the verdict and the action from the console alone.

## Positioning

It closes the liability gap for agent purchases: when an agent buys, it is otherwise unclear whether the user, the agent platform, or the merchant is at fault. Provenant answers from cryptographically verifiable evidence captured at purchase time, not reconstructed after the dispute. A model only reports structured differences between claim and snapshot; deterministic rules pick the verdict; nothing moves money without merchant approval (or an explicit AUTO_ACTION setting).

## Operating Context

The console sits beside PayPal's Disputes API. Disputes arrive by webhook or polling; the console shows the queue, the dispute detail, the liability ledger by agent platform, and a bundle explorer. Demo runs locally in mock PayPal mode (`pnpm demo`, five scripted scenarios) and optionally against the PayPal sandbox. Presented on laptops and projected in a recorded video.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five verdicts: WITHIN_MANDATE, AGENT_OUT_OF_BOUNDS, MERCHANT_MISREPRESENTED, SHARED_FAULT, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
- PayPal calls: provide-evidence (with PDF), provide-supporting-info, send-message, accept-claim, make-offer; gated by the dispute's HATEOAS links; ACTION_NOT_AVAILABLE when PayPal does not offer the call.
- Console uses the server API (`/v1/disputes`, `/v1/disputes/:id`, approve/reject, `/v1/liability`, `/v1/bundles/...`) with an admin or merchant key held server-side.
- Stack fixed: Next.js App Router, Tailwind 4, AG Grid Community for the dispute queue.
- Must read clearly on a projector and work at laptop width.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Provenant" only. No logo, colours or fonts committed. Must not use PayPal's logo or imply it is a PayPal product.

## Evidence on Hand

Real data comes from the five scenarios (remorse, overspend, wrong-item, ambiguous, tampered) plus any sandbox runs; product images are generated by the demo store. No customers, testimonials or metrics exist; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Every verdict shows its reasons and the evidence fields they cite.
2. The exact PayPal request is visible before anyone approves it.
3. Integrity is shown, not claimed: hashes, signatures and chain checks are inspectable.
4. Humans approve money movement; the model never decides.
